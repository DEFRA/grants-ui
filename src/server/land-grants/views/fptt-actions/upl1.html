UPL1: Moderate livestock grazing on moorland

What you must do to get paid for UPL1 and advice on how to do it.

Duration

12 months

How much you’ll be paid

£35 per hectare (ha) per year

Action’s aim

This action’s aim is that there’s a moderate livestock density grazing on the moorland, so its habitat is maintained or enhanced alongside farming.

What to do

You must make sure that the livestock density across the land entered into this action does not exceed 0.16 grazing livestock units (GLU) per hectare at any time.

{% include "fptt-actions/shared/glu-conversion.html" %}

Read the ‘advice to help you do UPL1’ to find out how to calculate the livestock density.

{% include "fptt-actions/shared/grazing-stocking-calendar.html" %}

If something happens which means you cannot complete this action, you must tell the Rural Payments Agency about this in writing as soon as possible. For example, if there are fodder supply issues, so you need to increase the stocking level.

Read section 13.2, ‘What you must do if you cannot comply with your agreement’ (opens in new tab) in the FPTT information to find out how to do this.

When to do it

You must do this action from the start date of your FPTT agreement.
