11. About your FPTT agreement

Your FPTT agreement is the legally binding agreement between you and Defra. It comprises:

  • your agreement document – you can view this in the Farm payments service
  • the FPTT terms and conditions
  • the FPTT actions you have selected

11.1 What you’re agreeing to do

When you enter an FPTT agreement, you’re agreeing to:

  • complete the FPTT actions you have selected, as referenced in your agreement document and set out in the FPTT actions at your agreement’s start date
  • comply with your agreement’s terms and conditions
  • obtain, maintain and comply with any required permits, licences, permissions, consents, approvals, certificates and authorisations (whether statutory or otherwise)

11.2 Agreement start and end date

Your agreement document will show the start and end dates for your agreement (the ‘agreement duration’).

The agreement start date will be on the first day of the calendar month after you have accepted your offer.

The agreement end date will be 12 months after the agreement start date (for example, if an agreement start date is 1 January 2026, then the agreement end date would be 31 December 2026).
