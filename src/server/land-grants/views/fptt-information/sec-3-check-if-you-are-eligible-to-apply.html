3. Check if you’re eligible to apply

You can apply for an FPTT agreement if:

  • you’re a farmer or land manager who has been invited to apply
  • you have management control of the land you want to enter into actions – read section 4: ‘Check if you have management control of land’ to find out what this means
  • your land is eligible for the actions you select
  • the land parcels you want to apply for are wholly located in England

3.1 If you’ve received a Lump Sum Exit Scheme payment

If you’ve received a payment under the Lump Sum Exit Scheme (opens in new tab) to leave or retire from farming:

  • as an individual – you’re not eligible to apply for an FPTT agreement, unless you repay the lump sum
  • as a partnership or limited company – if only some partners or shareholders left the business, the remaining partners or shareholders can apply for an FPTT agreement without repaying the lump sum

3.2 If you receive delinked payments

If you receive delinked payments (opens in new tab) (which replaced BPS payments from 2024), you can apply for an FPTT agreement.

3.3 Eligibility of public bodies

A public body is a formally established organisation that is publicly funded to provide a public or government service.

Public bodies are not eligible to apply for an FPTT agreement.

Public bodies include:

  • Crown bodies (including all government departments, executive agencies and trading funds)
  • non-departmental public bodies
  • local authorities
  • national park authorities
  • park corporations

See a list of all departments, agencies and public bodies (opens in new tab).

Parish councils and former college farms are not considered public bodies. They can apply for an FPTT agreement if they’re eligible.
