8. What to do before you apply for an FPTT agreement

Before you apply for an FPTT agreement, you need to complete the following steps.

8.1 Read the rules you must follow

Read the rules you must follow when you enter an FPTT agreement to make sure you understand what you’re agreeing to do.

You can find these rules in:

  • the FPTT actions you select – these are shown in the FPTT actions (opens in new tab), with the action’s requirements identified by a ‘must’
  • the FPTT terms and conditions (opens in new tab)

You also need to read the sections of this FPTT information which explain the rules set out in the FPTT agreement terms and conditions. These sections include:

  • section 1 (about the Farm payments technical test) – the information on FPTT actions
  • section 2 (when and how much you’ll get paid)
  • section 4 (check if you have management control of land)
  • section 5 (check if your land is eligible for FPTT actions)
  • section 10 (get all necessary regulatory consents, permissions and licences in place)
  • section 11 (about your FPTT agreement)
  • section 12 (managing your FPTT agreement)
  • section 13 (complying with your FPTT agreement)

8.2 Check your registered contact details are up to date

Check your registered contact details, including your business email address, are up to date (opens in new tab). The RPA will use these details to contact you about your FPTT application and agreement.

8.3 Check you have the correct permission to apply for FPTT

You need the correct permission to apply for a FPTT agreement for your business (SBI). Sign in to the Rural Payments service (opens in new tab).

From ‘Business overview’ select ‘Give people permission to act for this business.’ You need to have either:

  • ‘Business Details: Full’ permission
  • ‘FPTT: Submit’ permission

If you do not have either of these permissions, you will not be able to apply for FPTT for your business.

For FPTT you are not able to give anyone else permission to act on your behalf.

8.4 Check your registered land details are up to date

Check the registered land details on your digital maps are up to date, so they show:

  • all the land parcels you want to include in your FPTT application
  • the correct total area (in hectares) for each land parcel
  • the correct land covers for each land parcel (such as arable land, permanent grassland, permanent crops or relevant non-agricultural cover)

If these land details are not up to date, this may prevent you from applying for the FPTT actions you select. Read the guidance on how to check your digital maps (opens in new tab).

If you need to update your digital maps, read the guidance on:

  • how to update your digital maps online (opens in new tab)
  • updating your maps using form RLE1 (opens in new tab) (only if you’re unable to do this online)
