14. Consequences of expiry or termination of your Agreement

14.1 If we or you terminate the Agreement, or the Agreement expires on the Agreement End Date, this will not affect the rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities that either of us have accrued up to the point of termination or the Agreement End Date.

14.2 Termination or expiry of the Agreement will not affect our or your continuing rights and obligations under any condition in the Agreement which is intended to continue beyond the Agreement Period. This includes our obligation to pay the final quarterly instalment of the Grant provided that you have submitted your Annual Declaration and have not breached your Agreement.
