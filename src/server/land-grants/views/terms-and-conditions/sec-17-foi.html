17. Freedom of information (FOI)

17.1 You acknowledge that we are subject to the requirements of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (“FOIA”) and the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (“EIR”).

17.2 We may need to share information we hold about you or the Agreement so we or Defra can comply with any FOIA and EIR request.

The extent, content and format of the information we disclose is our decision. In making any disclosure, we will have due regard to Data Protection Legislation.

17.3 You will provide all necessary assistance and cooperation which we or Defra reasonably request for the purposes of complying with our obligations under FOIA and EIR within a time period specified by us. You must tell us as soon as reasonably practicable if you receive a FOIA or EIR request intended for us or Defra.
