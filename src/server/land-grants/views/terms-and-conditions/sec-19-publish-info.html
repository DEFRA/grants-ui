19. What information we may publish about you

19.1 We are required by Law to publish certain specified information about agreement holders who have received relevant Grant payments.

19.2 Condition 19.1 only applies if you have received a total value of relevant payments (as defined in the Regulations) equal to or more than £1,250 in the relevant financial year.

19.3 The specified information shall:

  1. be published on a searchable database available at https://find-farm-and-land-payment-data.defra.gov.uk/; and
  2. remain on that searchable database for three (3) years from initial publication.

19.4 If condition 19.1 applies to your Grant, we will publish the following specified information:

  1. your full name;
  2. the post town, and post code area and district where:
    1. you are resident, if you are a natural person;
    2. your registered office is located, if you are incorporated (for example, a company); or
    3. your primary base of business is located, if you are unincorporated (for example, a sole trader);
  3. the total Grant payments received by you in the relevant financial year; and
  4. a description of the activities financed by the Grant payments.
