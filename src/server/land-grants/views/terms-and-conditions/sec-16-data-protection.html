16. Data protection

16.1 We and you must always comply with our respective obligations under “Data Protection Legislation”. We may be required to provide personal information to Defra. We also are required by law to publish certain information about you, this is described in condition 19. Data Protection Legislation means (a) the UK GDPR, (b) the Data Protection Act 2018 to the extent that it relates to processing of personal data and privacy and (c) all applicable Law about the processing of personal data and privacy.

16.2 For information on how we handle personal data search for ‘Rural Payments Agency Personal Information Charter’ (opens in new tab) and our Privacy Policy on GOV.UK (opens in new tab).
